# 3ll

> Studio site for 3ll — a small design practice that builds tailor-made websites and applications. One page, no CMS: identity, live work, and a clear way to get in touch.

**Role:** Designer & front-end
**Type:** Studio marketing site
**Stack:** HTML, CSS, Google Fonts (Tektur, Zen Maru Gothic), SVG
**Links:** [Live](https://3ll.gr)

---

## Overview

3ll’s studio homepage is a calling card, not a funnel. It states who the practice is, lists live products with direct links, surfaces work-in-progress systems honestly, and points contact to email and Instagram only. No forms, newsletters, or lead capture — just a fast page that sends visitors out to real work.

---

## Highlights

- Opens with the studio logomark and a short positioning line
- Lists live products (Kardias, Brioche, Retrofolio) with direct links to explore
- Surfaces work-in-progress systems (PMS, FDS) as honest status, not oversold launches
- Contact via email and Instagram only — no forms or newsletters
- Desktop layout: identity on the left, horizontal product shelf on the right
- Stacks cleanly on smaller screens without rewriting the markup
- Single HTML document — no CMS or JS bundle

---

## Design

The site is intentionally sparse. Typography and the SVG mark do the branding work; products are a shelf of named pieces rather than a case-study grid. Dark navy palette with Tektur for headings and Zen Maru Gothic for body. The goal is a studio homepage that feels like a calling card — fast to load, easy to scan, and hard to confuse with a template.

---

## Build

One HTML document, two stylesheets, and static assets. No framework, no CMS, no JS bundle. Layout is CSS-driven: a split desktop composition that collapses into a readable stack on mobile. Shipping as static files keeps the studio site as light as the brand it represents.
